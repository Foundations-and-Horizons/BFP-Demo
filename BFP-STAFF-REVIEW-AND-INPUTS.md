# BFP — Staff review and outstanding inputs

**Purpose:** A focused review guide for Rebekah and individual staff meetings. This is a synthetic-data demonstration, not a production rollout or a request for confidential records. Current paid scope excludes volunteer management.

**Demo:** https://foundations-and-horizons.github.io/BFP-Demo/

## What to review independently before our meetings

1. **Rebekah — Board Reporting:** Choose a full calendar month and Bountiful location, inspect source-readiness warnings, edit narrative, choose report pages, preview and print/save a PDF. Tell us what sections, order, metrics and presentation must change. Please provide an example board packet with private data removed.
2. **Rebekah — Monthly Financials and Grants:** Check the proposed manual finance categories, location reporting, grants fields, correction notes and reporting deadlines. Confirm whether the board packet needs separate financial commentary or approval steps.
3. **Rachelle — Incoming/Outgoing Food and Inventory:** Walk through donor/receipt entry, transfers, Grocery Rescue reporting, physical count, truck load/return checks, evidence register and month-end review. Identify what is missing or incorrect before any inventory math is treated as official.
4. **Tryn — Off-site Services and Mini Market:** Check separate Mobile Truck, Mobile Bus and Mini Market reporting, locations/stops, and monthly totals. Confirm what is captured in each field and whether historical spreadsheets can be summarized without client-identifying information.
5. **Rebekah — Data Import Center:** Check the preview, duplicate/replacement behavior and CSV exports using fictional test rows. Confirm desired historical coverage and ownership of monthly source files.

## Suggested one-on-one sessions

- **Rebekah (45–60 min):** board packet, monthly reporting, finances, grants and final acceptance priorities.
- **Rachelle (45–60 min):** food receipts, transfer forms, inventory counts, truck returns and month-end reconciliation.
- **Tryn (30–45 min):** off-site services, Mini Market and reporting sheets.
- **Follow-up review (30 min):** resolve conflicts, approve definitions and identify any separately priced enhancements.

## Explicit exclusions and honest limitations

- **Volunteer management: Coming Soon, outside the paid work order.** Volunteer accounts, kiosk, PIN attendance, scheduling, directory, notifications and consent workflows require a separate funded agreement. Aggregate volunteer-hours reporting may remain as a community metric.
- The public demo uses fictional browser-local data. Staff should **not** enter real client, donor, volunteer or confidential information.
- No live PantrySoft or MealConnect integration is claimed.
- Role previews are not secure authentication; month-end reviews are not locks or audited approvals.
- Production hosting, shared database, authorization, backups, migration and handoff are a distinct implementation/acceptance stage, not completed by the GitHub Pages demo.
- Actual browser workflow testing and PDF pagination acceptance remain open.
